# hackrpi26
By Stanley Liu, Akshat Prakash, Evan Snyder, and James Tarantino

Our project aims to provide all transportation options to RPI students into one consolidated application. Right now the free options of transportation are divided between apps: the Shuttle, CDTA Navigator, CDPHP Cycle!, and the RPI Free and For Sale Discord as a last option for rideshares. This would allow students to learn every RPI-provided free mode of transport as well as combine multiple different modes, therefore increasing the coverage of the local area they can get to for free. We want students to explore their surroundings while also cutting down on their carbon dioxide emissions in the process.

The rideshare feature will enable drivers to send out alerts to students when they are about to leave to drive to a location. This can be used for daily tasks like groceries to coordinating leaving for holiday break at the Albany Train Station, Bus Terminal, or Airport. The advantage over using our project over other rideshare services is the ability to discuss fair prices beforehand: where the drivers won't get a cut taken from them, and the students won't be overpaying. It is reported that Uber takes 50-80% of the cut of the money that drivers make.
